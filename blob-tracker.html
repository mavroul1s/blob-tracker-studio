A quick field guide.

  1. Load a clip. Drop your video onto the canvas. MP4 and WebM work well; other formats depend on your browser.
  2. Find your subjects. Bright areas works for light subjects; Dark areas for dark subjects. Moving areas compares against a slowly adapting background and works best with a fixed camera.
  3. Tune the threshold. Use Detection mask to see exactly what is detected. Raise Minimum blob size to remove noise.
  4. Make it yours. Change boxes, labels, links, trails and video visibility. IDs follow nearby blobs; they can change when objects merge or overlap.
  5. Export. Render from the beginning at playback speed. Keep the tab visible. Download the finished video, or save a single frame.

Your video is read locally. Nothing is sent to a server.
